StageChart Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

StageChart is a chord chart, setlist and performance app. It is built so your music stays yours: the developer does not collect, see or sell any of your data.

What stays on your device

Your songs, charts, PDFs, markup, setlists, backing tracks and settings are stored on your device. StageChart has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it. The developer cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Band Sync

Band Sync sends the current song, and markup if you choose to share it, directly to bandmates' devices on your local network. Nothing passes through a server, and nothing is kept after the session ends.

Camera

Scan Paper uses the camera to photograph a printed chart. Text is recognised on your device; images are not uploaded.

Importing from a web page

When you import a chart from a web address, your device downloads that page directly. StageChart does not log or share the addresses you use.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. The developer receives no payment details.

Children

StageChart does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Questions: stagechart.app@gmail.com.
