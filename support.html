StageChart Support

Chord charts, setlists and Band Sync for gigging musicians.

Need help? Email stagechart.app@gmail.com. Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Common questions

How do I add my songs?

Tap + in the Library and choose Files (ChordPro, text or PDF), Paste Text, Scan Paper, From a Web Page, or Write a Chart. Charts with chords above the lyrics are converted automatically, with each chord placed over the right word.

How does Band Sync work?

Everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi. The leader opens Band and picks a setlist (leading needs Pro). Bandmates open Band, tap Look for Sessions Nearby, then Join; joining is free. Allow the Local Network prompt on each device. If a session doesn't appear, check that Local Network access is on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → StageChart.

Can I use a Bluetooth foot pedal?

Yes. Pair the pedal in the iPhone or iPad Settings → Bluetooth. Pedals that send arrow or page keys work: left goes back, right goes forward.

How do I sync my library between devices?

iCloud sync is part of StageChart Pro. Sign in to iCloud on each device, then turn on Settings → iCloud sync in StageChart. Songs, setlists, PDFs and markup sync to your own private iCloud storage.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Settings → Restore Purchases in StageChart.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In StageChart, go to Settings → Manage Subscription, or open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I export my songs?

Yes. Settings → Export library as ChordPro saves all your charts as one ChordPro file that works in other chord apps.
